Research at NEXCIV

Advancing civil engineering through interdisciplinary research, scientific inquiry, and innovative engineering perspectives.

Our Research Divisions

Areas of Research

NEXCIV brings together diverse areas of civil engineering to explore emerging challenges and develop research-driven solutions.

01 / DIVISION

Structural Engineering

Exploring structural behavior, performance, and innovative engineering systems through analytical, experimental, and computational approaches.

Research Focus

  • Structural behavior and performance evaluation
  • Advanced and hybrid structural systems
  • Finite Element Modeling and computational analysis
  • Innovative structural solutions
02 / DIVISION

Transportation Engineering

Investigating transportation systems, mobility challenges, and infrastructure performance to support efficient and sustainable transportation.

Research Focus

  • Traffic flow and operations
  • Transportation planning and mobility
  • Pavement materials and performance
  • Intelligent transportation systems
03 / DIVISION

Water Resources Engineering

Studying water systems and hydrological processes to address challenges related to water management, flooding, and hydraulic infrastructure.

Research Focus

  • Hydrology and hydraulics
  • Flood risk assessment
  • Water resources management
  • Sustainable water systems
04 / DIVISION

Construction Materials

Exploring material properties, innovative construction materials, and sustainable alternatives through scientific investigation and performance evaluation.

Research Focus

  • Concrete technology and characterization
  • Sustainable construction materials
  • Experimental material performance
  • Mechanical and durability properties
Our Approach

Research Philosophy

At NEXCIV, we believe meaningful engineering progress begins with curiosity, rigorous investigation, and collaboration. Our research approach emphasizes scientific understanding, critical evaluation, and the exploration of practical solutions to evolving civil engineering challenges.

Research at NEXCIV

Exploring Engineering Challenges Through Multidisciplinary Research

Our Research Divisions

NEXCIV explores emerging challenges in civil engineering through analytical investigation, computational approaches, experimental studies and interdisciplinary collaboration.

01. Structural Engineering

Investigating structural behavior, performance and innovative engineering systems.

Research Interests

  • Structural analysis and design
  • Computational and FEM-based modeling
  • Structural performance evaluation
  • Innovative and hybrid structural systems

02. Transportation Engineering

Exploring efficient, safe and sustainable transportation systems.

Research Interests

  • Traffic operations and management
  • Transportation planning
  • Pavement performance
  • Intelligent transportation systems

03. Water Resources Engineering

Addressing water-related engineering challenges through scientific investigation.

Research Interests

  • Hydrology and hydraulics
  • Flood risk assessment
  • Water resources management
  • Sustainable water systems

04. Construction Materials

Investigating material properties and advancing sustainable construction technologies.

Research Interests

  • Concrete technology
  • Sustainable construction materials
  • Material characterization
  • Experimental performance evaluation

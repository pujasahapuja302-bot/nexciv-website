Journal Article

Comparative Assessment of Structural Slab Systems in Mid-Rise Construction: Structural Performance Analysis Upon Load Response Using ETABS

International Journal of Scientific Research in Civil Engineering (IJSRCE)
Year: 2026
Authors: Puja Saha (Corresponding Author), Raisa Ferdousi, Tomal Roy, SK Labiba Amin, and Prof. Dr. Md. Mahmudur Rahman

Synopsis

Developed and comparatively analyzed ETABS models of a G+5 parking structure incorporating flat plate, one-way ribbed, and waffle slab systems under gravity and lateral loads, following BNBC 2020-based design criteria. The waffle slab achieved 25.61–34.00% lower slab deflection than the flat plate, while the one-way ribbed slab reduced concrete consumption by 14.53% and base shear by 24.88%, highlighting the trade-off between structural performance and material efficiency.

Conference Paper

Comparative Analysis of Traffic Flow Characteristics in Turbo-Roundabout and Conventional Roundabout: An Approach in VISSIM-Based Traffic Flow Simulation

8th International Conference on Civil Engineering for Sustainable Development (ICCESD 2026)
Year: 2026
Authors: Tomal Roy, Puja Saha (Second Author), Raisa Ferdousi, and Sadia Mohsin

Synopsis

Developed and calibrated a PTV VISSIM microsimulation model (GEH = 1.43) to compare conventional and turbo-roundabout performance under heterogeneous traffic conditions at Mirpur-10, Dhaka. The turbo-roundabout reduced average delay by 57%, travel time by 53%, and CO emissions by approximately 30%, demonstrating improved traffic efficiency.

Ongoing Research

Experimental Investigation of Sustainable Concrete Composites Incorporating Eggshell Powder and Jute Fiber: Mechanical Performance and Durability

Authors: Md. Rahatul Islam, Puja Saha, Imran Najir Shoun, Niloy Kumar Roy, and Saif Ahmed Santo

Synopsis

Experimentally investigated 135 concrete specimens incorporating eggshell powder (1.5%, 2.5%) and jute fibers (0.15%, 0.35%). Evaluated fresh, mechanical, and durability properties through slump, compressive strength (3, 14, 28 days), split tensile, flexural, water absorption, porosity, and electrical resistivity tests, with preliminary results showing up to 30.12% higher compressive strength.

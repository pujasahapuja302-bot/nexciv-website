About NEXCIV

NEXCIV (Next-Generation Civil Engineering Research Laboratory) is an independent multidisciplinary research initiative focused on exploring emerging challenges and advancing knowledge across diverse fields of civil engineering. Guided by academic mentorship and driven by research-oriented collaboration, NEXCIV aims to foster scientific inquiry, develop innovative engineering approaches, and contribute to sustainable infrastructure development.

Research Divisions

Structural Engineering

Structural behavior, computational modeling, performance evaluation and innovative structural systems.

Transportation Engineering

Traffic operations, transportation planning, pavement performance and intelligent mobility.

Water Resources Engineering

Hydrology, hydraulics, flood risk assessment and sustainable water management.

Construction Materials

Concrete technology, sustainable materials and experimental performance evaluation.

Our Vision

To become a recognized multidisciplinary research laboratory contributing to the advancement of civil engineering through innovative research, scientific excellence and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Our Mission

  • Promote research and innovation across civil engineering disciplines.
  • Encourage collaboration among students, researchers and academic professionals.
  • Explore engineering challenges through analytical, numerical and experimental investigations.
  • Contribute to sustainable and resilient infrastructure development.
  • Disseminate research findings through scholarly publications.
